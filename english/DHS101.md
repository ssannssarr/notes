# DHS101 — Communication & Language Notes

## 1. Skills

Skills are broadly divided into **Hard Skills** and **Soft Skills**.

### Hard Skills

Skills that can be gained through formal learning and experience.

- **Qualification**
- **Experience**
- **Diploma**

### Soft Skills

A set of skills that influence how one interacts with others.

Important soft skills:

- **Communication skills**
- **Interpersonal skills**
- **Leadership skills**
- **Teamwork**
- **Problem-solving skills**
- **Decision-making**
- **Time & stress management**
- **Innovation & creativity**

---

## 2. Communication Skills

Communication skills are divided into:

### Receptive Skills

Skills used for receiving information.

- **Listening**
- **Reading**

### Productive Skills

Skills used for producing information.

- **Speaking**
- **Writing**

## 3. Stress

### Formula

$$
S > P > R
$$

Where:

- **S = Stress**
- **P = Pressure**
- **R = Resources**

### Key Point

> Stress occurs when pressure is bigger than the available resources.

## 5. Word Formation

A word can be divided into:

1. **Root / Stem**
2. **Prefix**
3. **Suffix**

### Content Words vs Functional Words

#### Content Words

- Carry meaning by themselves.

#### Functional Words

Examples:

- `an`
- `in`
- `are`
- `have`
- `has`
- `shall`
- `will`

These are grammatical words.

### Example: Misinformation

```text
misinformation
├── mis-       → Prefix
├── inform     → Root
└── -ation     → Suffix
```

11. Subject–Verb Agreement

Meaning

Subject–verb agreement means that the subject and the verb in a sentence must agree in number and person.

Example

> The boy plays football.



The boy   → Subject
plays     → Verb
football  → Object

The singular subject boy agrees with the singular verb plays.
